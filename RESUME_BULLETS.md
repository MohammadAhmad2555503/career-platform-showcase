# Resume Bullets: Career Platform

- Built a production-oriented personal career platform with Next.js App Router, TypeScript, Tailwind CSS, Supabase, Resend, global search, SEO routes, contact handling, and private CMS foundations.
- Designed a normalized Supabase schema with RLS-enabled tables for profile settings, projects, articles, CVs, career data, professional links, contact messages, media, relationships, and analytics events.
- Implemented privacy-aware seed content, admin-only content management foundations, validated contact forms, honeypot/rate limiting, database inbox capture, and email delivery integration.
